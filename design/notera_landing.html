How it works

Three steps. That's genuinely it.

No new hardware, no template-building weekend. Open Notera, talk, done.

1

Hit record and talk

Have the visit exactly like you always do. Notera runs quietly in the background — it's fine with accents, interruptions, and the patient's own words.

2

Read the draft

Seconds after you stop, a structured SOAP note is waiting — subjective, objective, assessment and plan, in the order you'd write it yourself.

3

Tweak & sign off

Skim it, fix a word if you want, and copy it straight into your EMR. Most notes need barely a touch.

What makes it different

Built to be trusted with a real chart

Speed is easy. Getting it right, every time, is the hard part — and it's the part we obsess over.

Grounded, not guessed

It won't invent a lab it never heard

Every sentence Notera writes is traceable back to something that was actually said in the room. If a value wasn't mentioned, it doesn't appear — no hallucinated potassium, no phantom medications.

  • Line-by-line grounding to the transcript
  • Drug names checked against a real drug database
  • Flags anything it isn't sure it heard right
Sounds like you

Notes in your voice, in your specialty

Notera figures out the type of visit on its own and shapes the note the way your field actually documents — a cardiology plan reads like cardiology, a psych note reads like psych. No settings to fiddle with.

  • Edit once — it remembers your style
