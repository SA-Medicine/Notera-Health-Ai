Where you are, and where this takes you

The current UI works, but it reads as an internal tool: flat buttons, hard system fonts, abrupt state changes, no motion, and one visual language stretched across marketing, app and auth. For a multi-user SaaS competing in the ambient-scribe segment, first impression is the sales pitch — and trust is the whole game in healthcare.

The strategy is a deliberate split personality: the marketing site should be bold, animated and confident (it sells); the Scribe should be quiet, clean and clinical (it earns trust during real work). One shared token system keeps them siblings, not strangers.

Button component (the #1 fix)

Replace the single flat button with one component, 4 variants × 3 sizes × 6 states. Clear hierarchy is the biggest instant upgrade.

VariantUse forRest → Hover → Active
PrimaryThe one main action per view (Start free, Create SOAP)Gradient + glow → lift 2px, glow grows → scale .98
SecondarySupporting action (Watch demo, Copy)White/1px border → bg tint, lift → scale .98
GhostTertiary / in-toolbarTinted fill → deepen tint
DangerDestructive (Delete consult)Red → darken; always needs confirm dialog
✓ Do
Exactly one Primary per screen. Give every button a 150ms hover + a pressed state. Keep labels verb-first.
✗ Don't
Two identical-looking buttons side by side. "Submit"/"OK" labels. Instant, feedback-less clicks.
Marketing Homepage · Bold, animated SaaS

Landing page — section by section

Goal: a visiting clinician goes from "what is this" to "start free" in one scroll. Dark, confident, animated — the live mockup (notera_hero_mockup.html) shows the hero, trust bar and feature grid rendered.

1 · Sticky glass nav

Layout: logo left · Product/Features/Security/Pricing center · Sign in (ghost) + Start free (primary) right. Transparent on load, gains a blur + hairline border after 20px scroll.

Motion: backdrop-blur fade-in on scroll (250ms). Copy: keep nav to 4 items max.

Motion: staggered rise-in (headline → sub → CTAs, 60ms apart). Animated gradient-orb mesh drifting behind. A "browser frame" product card decks in with a 3D tilt and the note types itself line-by-line.

Alternatives for the headline — A (bold): "Stop typing notes. Start seeing patients." · B (outcome): "Finish your notes before the patient leaves." · C (calm/clinical): "Accurate clinical notes, written as you talk." Pick by brand voice; A/B convert, C reassures.

Recommended imagery (Unsplash — direct URLs)

Use warm, real-clinician photography (not stocky handshakes). Duotone them toward the brand for cohesion: photo → 12% brand overlay, slight desaturate. Source: unsplash.com (free, commercial-ok). Swap IDs freely — search "doctor tablet", "clinician patient", "medical team".

3 · Trust bar (social proof)

Immediately under the hero: 4 count-up stats — 98% note accuracy · 12 min saved/visit · 7 sec to first draft · 40k+ notes generated — plus a row of specialty logos. Animate the numbers counting up on scroll-into-view.

4 · How it works (3 steps)

Record → Review → Sign. Three cards with an animated connector line that draws in on scroll. Copy: "Hit record and talk normally." / "Notera drafts a structured SOAP note in seconds." / "Skim, edit inline, copy to your EMR."

5 · Feature grid

3×2 cards with a cursor-following glow and lift-on-hover (in the mockup). Lead with outcomes: Notes in seconds · Grounded & safe (no fabricated labs) · Specialty auto-tuned · Works with any accent · One-click EMR copy · Your data, encrypted.

6 · Security / trust band

Dark band with lock iconography: HIPAA-ready, encryption at rest & in transit, no training on your data, audit trail. Healthcare buyers scan for this before anything else — give it real estate.

Scribe App · Clean clinical trust

Accessibility audit & targets

Edge cases

  • Long note → scrolls within the card; sticky toolbar stays.
  • Slow generation → staged loader continues past last step with "still working…"; never a dead spinner.
  • Image fails → gradient placeholder, never a broken-image icon.
  • Reduced motion → all transforms become simple opacity fades.
