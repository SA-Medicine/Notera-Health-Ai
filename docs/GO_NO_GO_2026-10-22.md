# Go / no-go: Notera note pipeline (Style Spec v2) — 2026-10-22

## Verdict
**NO-GO for unsupervised use. GO for a supervised pilot** with the training doctor, every note read and signed by a clinician.
Reason: the pipeline is clearly better than the one it replaced on the measured points, but it has only been checked on 30 transcripts by an AI auditor, no clinician has reviewed the latest notes, and known error classes remain.

Last change (plan attribution + other-people filter): paired A/B, 3 pairs, audit **+1.1** (pairs +0.4 / +1.3 / +1.5), time **+3 %** → no measurable difference, kept. In 90 notes it moved 39 plan items (18 notes) and removed 18 lines about other people (10 notes); one wrong removal found and fixed.
Condition guard (remove unsaid diseases): paired A/B audit **-1.9** → rolled back (off by default).

## Not solved (do not hide these from the clinicians)
* Merged problems and plan items under the wrong problem (the auditor still flags both in most notes).
* Wrong drug names or results that come from the **recording** (Wegovy vs Zepbound; "you passed" vs positive FIT).
* Left/right is only **flagged**, never corrected. Ambiguous audio can give a wrong body site.
* The grounding agent can remove a true fact that the transcript words differently; it can leave a diagnosis that was implied, not said.
* About 3 "please check against the recording" flags per note.
* The doctor profile's four contradictions (refills, PMH length, one line per lab, note length) were kept as in v1 — not decided by the doctor.

## Before real doctors (required)
- [ ] Training doctor reviews **30–50 real notes** side by side with her own, and signs off the Advice / Follow-up lines and the v2 layout.
- [ ] The app shows the note as a **draft**, with the "please check" flags visible at the top.
- [ ] Pilot users told: read every line; check drug names, doses, side, results.
- [ ] Owner names who watches the Monitor daily for the first week (errors, 429s, timing).
- [ ] Rollback known: `GROUNDING=0`, `PLAN_SWEEP=0`, `ADVICE_LINES=0`, `QUALITY_GUARDS=0`, `ATTRIBUTION=0`, `THIRD_PARTY=0`, `CONDITION_GUARD=0` on the VM; Style Spec v1 prompt kept in `prompts/writer-v4.txt`.
- [ ] Gemini quota: lab testing shares production's quota. No big lab runs during clinic hours.

## Stop rules for the pilot
Stop and roll back if a clinician finds: a wrong drug, dose or side that reached a signed note; a missing plan; or more than 1 in 10 notes needing a major rewrite. Review after the first 50 notes.
